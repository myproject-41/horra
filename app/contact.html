Support

For product support, account questions, technical issues, or general assistance:

contact@horra-app.com

What to include

To help us respond faster, please include:

  • Your name
  • The email address associated with your Horra account, if applicable
  • A short description of the issue
  • Relevant screenshots, if helpful
  • Your device or browser information, if the issue is technical

Please do not send passwords, authentication codes, payment card numbers, or other sensitive credentials by email.
