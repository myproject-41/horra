Privacy Policy

Effective March 23, 2026 · Last updated September 22, 2026

Horra is a message-writing app. It helps you compose a personalized message and hand it off to WhatsApp to send. Horra does not host chats, deliver messages on your behalf, or store the conversation that follows.

This policy explains what Horra, a product of Amonrau Global LLC ("Amonrau Global," "we," "us," or "our"), collects when you use the app, how it's used, and the choices available to you.

Information we collect

  • Recipient details — the name, phone number, and relationship you enter, or import from your device contacts with your permission, to personalize a message.
  • Sender details — your name and, for reminders, the occasions and dates you save.
  • Message inputs — the occasion, tone (warm, short, formal, playful), and any details you add, which are used to generate the message text.
  • Location & language — your selected country and language, used to surface relevant local and global holidays.
  • Usage information — basic technical data such as device type and app interactions, collected to keep the app working and secure.

Contacts access

Importing a contact is optional. If you grant access, Horra reads the selected contact's name and number to fill in the recipient fields — it does not scan or upload your full address book.

How messages are generated and sent

The details you provide are processed by our AI systems to draft a message. When you tap send, Horra hands the message and recipient to WhatsApp through its standard share function — Horra does not access your WhatsApp account, read your WhatsApp messages, or store what happens after the message is sent. Horra is not affiliated with WhatsApp Inc. or Meta Platforms, Inc.

Reminders

If you save a reminder for a future occasion, we store the recipient, occasion, and date so we can prompt you when it's coming up.

How we use this information

  • To generate and personalize messages
  • To surface relevant occasions and reminders
  • To operate, secure, and improve the app
  • To meet legal and regulatory obligations

How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with service providers that support core functions (such as AI message generation and app hosting), or where required by law.

Data retention

We keep recipient and message details only as long as needed to provide the app's features, such as active reminders, or as required by law.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your information, or to withdraw contacts permission at any time in your device settings. Contact us using the details below to make a request.

Children's privacy

Horra is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
