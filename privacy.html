2. PRIVACY PRACTICES OF HORRA

Horra is engineered on a privacy-by-design and data-minimization principle. You are not required to register a formal user account, create a public profile, or submit your full legal name, residential address, or government-issued identification to use the core message-drafting features of the Horra mobile application.

  1. No Sale of Personal Data. We do not sell, rent, lease, or trade your personal information to data brokers, advertisers, or third parties for monetary or other valuable consideration.
  2. User-Controlled Message Delivery. Horra helps you draft personalized messages for meaningful occasions. You retain complete editorial control over every message. 4. ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE (AI) PROCESSING

4.1. How AI Message Drafting Works

Horra utilizes automated artificial intelligence and large language model (LLM) technologies to transform your selected occasion, relationship parameters, and custom notes into a cohesive message draft.

  1. Transient Processing: The contextual inputs you enter are transmitted securely via encrypted API connections to our backend infrastructure and designated AI processing providers solely to generate and return your requested message draft.
  2. No Foundation Model Training on Personal Prompts: We do not use your personal message prompts or custom edits to train public, third-party foundation AI models, nor do we publish or expose your drafted messages to other users.

4.2. How the WhatsApp Integration Works

Horra is designed to work alongside WhatsApp while keeping the final step strictly in your hands:

  1. Deep-Link / Intent Handoff: Once you review, regenerate, or manually edit your drafted message inside Horra and tap to send via WhatsApp, the App uses standard operating system sharing intents or official WhatsApp URL schemes to pass the text string into your locally installed WhatsApp application.
  2. No Access to WhatsApp Accounts or Chats: Horra does not have access to your WhatsApp account credentials, your private WhatsApp message history, your end-to-end encrypted chats, or your device's contact book unless you explicitly grant system-level permission or select a recipient yourself within WhatsApp. Once you transition into the WhatsApp application, your activity is governed exclusively by WhatsApp's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
