Privacy Policy

Effective March 23, 2026 · Last updated March 23, 2026

Horra (“Horra”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Horra collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website and services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Horra, we may collect:

  • Name and contact information you provide
  • Information about the person or occasion you are creating a message for
  • Message preferences, relationship details, occasion, tone, and other information you provide
  • Information required to provide our WhatsApp messaging functionality
  • Basic technical information such as browser, device, and usage information

We only collect information reasonably necessary to provide and improve Horra.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Generate personalized messages
  • Provide and operate Horra's services
  • Send messages through supported WhatsApp functionality when you request it
  • Improve reliability, security, and user experience
  • Respond to support requests
  • Prevent abuse, fraud, or unauthorized use

3. AI Message Generation

Horra uses artificial intelligence to help generate personalized messages based on information you provide. AI-generated content may not always be perfect, so you should review a message before sending it.

4. WhatsApp

When you choose to send a message through WhatsApp, the message and necessary information may be processed by WhatsApp/Meta and are also subject to their applicable terms and privacy policies.

Horra does not control WhatsApp's independent processing of information.

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information with service providers that help us operate Horra, such as hosting, analytics, AI, security, and messaging providers. These providers may process information only as necessary to provide their services.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain security.

8. Your Choices

You may contact us to ask questions about your information or request deletion where applicable.

9. Children's Privacy

Horra is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised “Last Updated” date.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Horra's privacy practices, contact:

Email: contact@horra-app.com
