Privacy Policy

Effective March 23, 2026 · Last updated September 22, 2026

Horra is a message-writing product. It helps you compose a personalized message and hand it off to WhatsApp to send. Horra is not a hosted chat service.

This policy explains what Horra, a product of Amonrau Global LLC (“Amonrau Global,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), collects when you use the product, how it is used, and choices that may be available to you.

Information we collect

  • Recipient details — information you enter to personalize a message, such as a name, phone number or relationship.
  • Message inputs — occasion, tone and other details you provide for message generation.
  • Preferences — selected country or language where used to surface relevant occasions.
  • Usage information — basic technical and interaction information needed to operate, secure and improve the product.

Contacts

If a version of Horra offers contact importing, that access is optional and subject to the permission shown by your device. Horra should request only the information needed for the selected recipient workflow.

Message generation and WhatsApp

The information you provide may be processed by service providers used for AI message generation. When you choose to send, Horra hands the prepared message to WhatsApp through the supported sending/share flow. Horra does not represent itself as WhatsApp or Meta and does not host your WhatsApp conversations.

How we use information

  • To generate and personalize messages.
  • To provide product features such as occasions and reminders.
  • To operate, secure and improve Horra.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers that support core product functions, or when required by law.

Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably needed for the applicable product feature, legitimate business purpose or legal requirement. Specific retention periods may vary by feature and service provider.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete certain information. You may also be able to withdraw device permissions through your device settings.

Children

Horra is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
