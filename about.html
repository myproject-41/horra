ABOUT HORRA

Helping people find the right words for meaningful moments.

A little context about the product behind the experience.

Horra is an AI-powered messaging companion created to make personal communication easier and more meaningful.

With Horra, you choose the occasion, select the person you want to reach, and share a little context about the relationship or moment. Horra then helps create a personalized message that feels natural and appropriate. You can review and edit the message before sending it through WhatsApp.

Horra is designed to help people express themselves — from birthdays and anniversaries to celebrations, encouragement, and everyday meaningful moments.

Our Company

Horra is a product operated by AMONRAU GLOBAL LLC, a registered business entity.

Horra operates independently as a brand and product and is not owned or operated by WhatsApp or Meta.

Our Mission

We believe that sometimes the hardest part of reaching out is knowing what to say.

Horra exists to make those moments easier — helping people turn their thoughts and feelings into words they can confidently share.
